Problem

Indian businesses pay for sales leads, then call them back too late, in the wrong language.

  • A buyer fills a Meta ad form at 9pm. The callback comes the next afternoon. By then they've spoken to a competitor.
  • Sales teams can't cover nights, weekends or a spike of 500 leads after a campaign.
  • Customers switch between Hindi, Telugu and English mid-sentence. Most voice bots handle only one language.
Tell this as a story about one buyer. If anyone on the team has seen it happen (a family member buying a flat, a friend's showroom), use that.

How it works

A real phone call, turned into speech, reasoning and a spoken reply in real time.

01 · Phone
Twilio & Exotel
Indian numbers through Exotel, international through Twilio, streamed live.
02 · Listen
Deepgram & Sarvam
Speech-to-text tuned for Indian languages and code-switching.
03 · Think
Gemini + LangGraph
An industry conversation graph, searching your brochures in Pinecone.
04 · Speak
Natural voice reply
Callers can interrupt any time, like a real conversation.

Why it's different

Indian buyers don't speak one language per call. Our agents switch languages when the caller does.

  • Each industry gets its own conversation flow (pitch, objections, booking), not one generic prompt.
  • We rebuilt product search for Indian languages after off-the-shelf models failed on Hindi and Telugu questions.
The search story is a real finding: the first embedding model scored about 0.31 similarity on Hindi and Telugu questions, so the team moved to gemini-embedding-001. Tell it if a judge asks about the hardest problem.
